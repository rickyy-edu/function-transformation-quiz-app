HKUGA UT & HKDSE Transformation Reference Guide

Key rules, transformation order guidelines, and traps extracted directly from school Unit Tests.

1. Translations
  • $y = f(x) + k$: Translated UPWARDS by $k$ units ($k>0$).
  • $y = f(x) - k$: Translated DOWNWARDS by $k$ units ($k>0$).
  • $y = f(x - h)$: Translated RIGHTWARDS by $h$ units ($h>0$).
  • $y = f(x + h)$: Translated LEFTWARDS by $h$ units ($h>0$).
2. Reflections
  • $y = -f(x)$: Reflected with respect to the $x$-axis ($y$-values change sign).
  • $y = f(-x)$: Reflected with respect to the $y$-axis ($x$-values change sign).
3. Enlargement & Period
  • $y = a \cdot f(x)$: Vertically enlarged to $a$ times the original (Amplitude changes for trig).
  • $y = f(b \cdot x)$: Horizontally compressed to $\frac{1}{b}$ times the original (Period becomes $\frac{360^\circ}{b}$).
HKUGA School UT Trap: Does Order Matter?

Consider obtaining $g(x) = f(2x + 6)$ from $f(x)$:

Method A (Factorize first): $f(2(x+3))$
  1. Translate leftwards by 3 units: $f(x+3)$
  2. Compress horizontally to $\frac{1}{2}$ times: $f(2x+3)$
Method B (Horizontal enlargement first): $f(2x)$
  1. Compress horizontally to $\frac{1}{2}$ times: $f(2x)$
  2. Translate leftwards by 6 units: $f(2(x+3)) = f(2x+6)$
